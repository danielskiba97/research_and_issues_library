# D-008 — Narrow-column resize tooltip differs (Monday shows "Resize Column", Pipeline does not)

**Category:** Visual / Hover affordance
**Related states:** S22 (narrow column truncation)
**Related test cases:** TC-025

## Summary
When hovering the column resize handle of a narrowed Status column, Monday shows a "Resize Column" tooltip. Pipeline shows no tooltip on the splitter.

## Monday behaviour
At a narrowed column the long label chip truncates with an ellipsis and a "Resize Column" tooltip appears at the drag handle.

## Pipeline behaviour
The long label chip truncates ("zz_very_lo..."), the header truncates ("S..."), and the summary chip narrows, but no "Resize Column" tooltip appears on the splitter.

## Reproduction steps
1. Narrow the Status column to ~100px.
2. Hover the right edge (resize handle) of the column header.
3. Observe tooltip presence.

## Expected result (Monday reference)
"Resize Column" tooltip appears at the drag handle.

## Actual Pipeline result
No tooltip appears on the splitter.
